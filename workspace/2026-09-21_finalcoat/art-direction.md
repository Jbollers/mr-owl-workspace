# Art Direction

Owner-supplied direction: stylized 3D psychological horror rather than full photorealism.

## Shape and materials

- Slightly exaggerated proportions and silhouettes.
- Simplified but recognizable objects.
- Chunky furniture and architectural shapes.
- Soft bevels instead of perfectly sharp edges.
- Hand-painted or lightly distressed materials.
- Heavy environmental wear without excessive visual clutter.

## Color and light

Muted blue-gray, green, brown and charcoal form the base palette. Warm orange tungsten contrasts with cold moonlight. Reserve deep red for warnings and supernatural details.

The apartment is warmer and safer than customer properties, but never completely comfortable. Subtle horror enters the hub as the story progresses.

## Horror through contrast

- Familiar objects placed incorrectly.
- Warm rooms containing unnaturally cold shadows.
- Clean paint covering damaged or disturbing surfaces.
- Human-shaped negative spaces.
- Brief silhouettes instead of clearly visible monsters.
- Movement at the edge of the player's view.
- Sound suggesting activity in inaccessible rooms.

Initially, the player should question whether disturbances come from shadows, fumes, exhaustion and poor lighting.

## Contractor portal

Worn industrial interface: dark blue-green panels, orange selection highlights, off-white distressed icons, scanlines and dirt overlays, utilitarian typography, minimal animation and occasional visual interference.

## Proposed reference presentation

Use clear construction and consistent proportions for modeling views. Keep dramatic lighting studies separate when shadows obscure shape. Match wear and major details across views of an approved asset. These are production recommendations; no images have been generated or approved.
