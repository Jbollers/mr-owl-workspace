# Concept Plan

## 1. Define one asset

Choose the first character, prop or environment element. Describe its role in the game, overall shape, materials, approximate scale and required moving parts. Supply existing references when available.

## 2. Agree on the visual direction

Record the desired style and a short list of features to preserve. Choose any generation tool and budget before starting a paid job. No provider is connected by this project setup.

## 3. Review the concept

Check silhouette, proportions, construction and gameplay needs. Keep related revisions as tabs in this card. Label the selected image as approved only after the owner's review.

## 4. Prepare modeling references

Use the approved design to prepare the views and close-ups the modeler needs. Keep proportions, materials and details consistent across views. Record unresolved details rather than inventing hidden construction.

## 5. Hand off to modeling

Keep the approved concept, reference views and asset notes together. Confirm the modeling software, engine, dimensions and export requirements before producing a model.
