# Asset Library

Inventory supplied by the owner. These are planned assets, not completed models.

## Living area

Worn couch and mismatched cushions; small coffee table; cheap television or old monitor; uneven rug; moving boxes; shoes near the entrance; backpack or work bag; floor lamp; framed photographs; unopened mail; takeout containers; wall clock; paint color samples; contractor advertisements.

## Kitchen

Aging refrigerator; small stove and oven; worn cabinets; sink with dishes; kettle and mugs; cheap microwave; trash can; cleaning products; grocery bags; notes or overdue bills on the refrigerator; one flickering overhead light.

## Bedroom

Unmade king-size bed; bedside table; phone charger; laundry basket; clothing storage; guitar case; alarm clock; curtains or blinds; personal photographs; boxes that have never been unpacked.

## Bathroom

Tub and shower; sink and damaged mirror; toilet; bathroom rug; towel holder; medicine cabinet; cleaning chemicals; water-stained ceiling; weak or flickering light; ventilation grate that occasionally produces strange sounds.

## Office and contractor area

Computer desk; monitor displaying the contractor portal; keyboard and mouse; office chair; job documents; paint receipts; customer notes; calendar; map of completed jobs; contractor ID card; phone or answering machine; locked filing cabinet; printer; paint-company posters.

## Painting equipment

Paint roller; roller extension pole; paintbrushes in several sizes; paint buckets; paint trays; drop cloths; masking tape; putty knife; scraper; sandpaper; spackle container; step ladder; work gloves; respirator mask; flashlight; portable work light; tool bag; cleaning rags; plastic sheeting; paint-stained clothing; color swatches; moisture meter; empty solvent containers.

Possible progression: larger roller, brighter work light, faster paint mixer, additional paint capacity and tools that reveal hidden wall damage. These remain possibilities rather than fixed requirements.

## Horror props and environmental clues

- Chains and multiple locks on an interior door.
- Furniture facing a blank wall.
- Family photographs with one figure scratched out.
- Paintings hung backward.
- Repeated contractor symbols.
- Wet footprints without a visible source.
- Paint rollers that move when unattended.
- Handprints beneath fresh paint.
- Claw marks hidden behind furniture.
- Old newspaper clippings.
- Missing-person flyers.
- Customer instructions that become increasingly strange.
- Mirrors reflecting a figure absent from the room.
- Vents releasing dark smoke.
- Radios producing distorted conversations.
- Clocks stopping at the same time.
- Paint buckets filled with an unidentified dark substance.
- Recently occupied-looking rooms despite years of abandonment.
- Previous painters' notes hidden inside walls.

## Proposed first asset

**Paint roller, tray and bucket** would make a small starting set tied directly to the core gameplay. It can establish shape, wear, materials and scale before larger furniture or room concepts. This is a recommendation, not a selected or approved asset.
