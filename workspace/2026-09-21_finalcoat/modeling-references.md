# Modeling References

No references have been approved yet.

For the first asset, collect the approved concept, useful front/side/back views, material details, scale notes and moving-part requirements. The views needed depend on the asset; a simple prop may need fewer than a character.

Keep source files under `projects/finalcoat/`. Put reference images and readable results in this card as they become available. Images should support full-size viewing and download; videos should have playback controls.

## Ready-for-modeling check

- The owner has selected the design.
- Shapes and proportions agree across reference views.
- Materials and important details are identified.
- Scale, intended use and moving parts are clear.
- Unresolved areas are documented.

Concept images are visual references. A finished 3D asset still requires modeling and any needed topology, UV, material, rigging and export work.
